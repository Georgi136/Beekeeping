# SAKI - Beekeeping Shop Dupnitsa

## General Information

SAKI is a family beekeeping business with over 20 years of experience in beekeeping and natural honey production. The company is based in Dupnitsa, Bulgaria, and offers a full range of beekeeping products and equipment.

## Main Products and Services

### Bee Products
- Natural honey from our own apiaries
- Propolis balm

### Beekeeping Equipment
- Hives and frames (Dadant, Langstroth, Universal)
- Wax foundations
- Protective gear (suits, gloves, nets)
- Honey extractors (manual and electric)
- Beekeeping tools (knives, forks, smokers)
- Feeding syrups

## Beekeeping Farms

The company manages its own apiaries in the villages of:
- **Dolistovo** - known region with rich flower diversity
- **Korkina** - clean environment, ideal for beekeeping

These regions provide perfect conditions for producing pure, natural honey without chemicals or additives.
